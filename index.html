About IAS

Innovative Assessment Solutions (IAS) is a premier psychometric and educational measurement consulting firm. We bring together leading experts in the field to provide rigorous, research-based solutions for assessment design, validation, and reporting.

Our team specializes in applying modern psychometric methods — including item response theory, diagnostic classification models, and Bayesian analysis — to real-world assessment challenges faced by educational agencies, credentialing organizations, and research institutions.

Our Services

📈

Psychometric Consulting

Expert guidance on test development, validation, and standard setting. We provide rigorous psychometric analysis aligned with professional standards (AERA, APA, NCME).

📋

Test Development

End-to-end test development services including item writing, field testing, item bank management, and automated test assembly for licensure, certification, and educational assessments.

📊

Data Analysis & Reporting

Advanced statistical analysis including IRT calibration, equating, linking, and DIF analysis. Clear, actionable reports for stakeholders at all technical levels.

📚

Research & Development

Applied and methodological research in educational measurement, cognitive diagnostic modeling, and next-generation assessment design.

🏫

Training & Workshops

Professional development workshops and university-level instruction in psychometric methods, R programming for measurement, and modern test theory.

⚙

Software & Tools

Development of open-source R packages and custom software solutions for measurement practitioners, educators, and researchers.
